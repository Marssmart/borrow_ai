Playback and capture controls

Playback and recording controls on Android. The playback panel ships in phase 1. Speed, interruptions, recording pause and resume, and duration labels are design mockups. Full spec: Playback and capture controls design.

Shipped The playback panel (seek bar, skips of 15 seconds, play or pause) and the list-card Pause state are built in phase 1. Mockup Dashed outlines mark what is still a mockup: speed, the interruption notice, recording pause, and the duration label. Android only The playback controls are Android only. iOS keeps the play and stop button. Background playback with notification controls is a later phase (P5), not shown here.

Screen · 1 · Playback panel

Seek, skip, pause, and speed Shipped Speed and notice: mockup Android only

The panel in the detail view shows elapsed and total time, a seek bar, skips of 15 seconds, one play or pause button, and the speed. Speed is chosen from a short menu. Shipped: the seek bar, the times, the skips, and play or pause. Dashed outlines mark the speed chip, the speed menu, and the interruption notice, which come in phase 2.

Screen · 2 · Legacy memories

Duration unknown, never an estimate Shipped Speed: mockup Android only

A memory saved before this release has no stored duration. Idle memories show "Duration unknown" until P3 stores durations. While a memory plays or is paused, its duration comes from the player, and the seek bar and skips work then. Play works from the start. The speed chip is a mockup until phase 2.

Mockup · 3 · Recording

Pause, resume, and the notification Mockup Android only

Pause appears only in tap-to-record. The elapsed time counts active recording only, and the live transcript and the notification both show the paused state. Hold-to-record has no pause control.

Screen · 4 · List cards

Play or pause, and the duration label Pause shipped Duration label: mockup Android only

The list card shows Pause while its memory plays. A duration label appears only when the duration is known. A legacy memory shows no label, so there is no invented total. Shipped: the Pause state. Dashed: the duration label, which comes in phase 3.
