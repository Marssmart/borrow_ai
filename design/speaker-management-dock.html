BorrowAI · navigation concept

Make voices as easy to revisit as memories.

A dedicated Speakers destination gives voice identity a clear home without interrupting recording or burying everyday management inside Settings.

01

Four peer destinations

Keep Record and Memories in their current dock positions; add Speakers beside them, with Settings remaining the home for consent, availability, and model setup.

02

Library first, detail in context

Search and select a named voice in place. Its sample, linked-memory count, recent recordings, rename, and merge actions are immediately visible—no hidden settings route.

03

Make voice data legible

Show local-only sample handling near the library, and use linked memories as the path back to context. Naming a voice remains a label, not a claim of verified identity.

Visual direction follows the existing BorrowAI light palette, teal accent, soft translucent panels, rounded floating dock, and the current speaker color family. The prototype is interactive; actions are illustrative and do not modify app data.
